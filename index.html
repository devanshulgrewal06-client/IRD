ABOUT IRD INFRATECH

Engineering with purpose. Building with responsibility.

IRD Infratech Private Limited is focused on construction and infrastructure work, with a commitment to quality and timely execution.

IRD Infratech Private Limited was established in 2024 in Modinagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The company introduces itself as an infrastructure developer providing civil construction services and contributing to roads, warehouses, boundary walls, protection walls and water-supply connections.

CAPABILITIES

What we do

Specialized construction and infrastructure services delivered with quality, safety and timely execution.

01

RE Wall Work

Construction and installation of reinforced earth walls using T-shape RE panels for slope stabilization and land retention.

02

Pre-Cast Boundary Wall Work

Supply and installation of durable pre-cast boundary walls for secure and long-lasting site protection.

03

Structure Work

R.O.B., M.N.B., flyovers, box-culverts and other major civil and structural infrastructure works.

04

Pre-Cast Crash Barrier

Manufacturing and installation of pre-cast crash barriers for highway safety, traffic separation and protection.

05

Fibre Cable Work

OFC and fibre-cable infrastructure work including trenching, ducting, laying and road-corridor reinstatement.
